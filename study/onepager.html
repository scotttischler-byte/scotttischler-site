The AI Recommendation Study: What AI Actually Recommends

We asked an AI engine 8 real "what's the best…" questions and recorded exactly what it recommended. Every figure below is counted from a real response — nothing estimated.
The headline: Search gave users ten links and let them choose. AI gives a short list and does the choosing. If you're not on it, you're invisible — there is no page two.
~6
Businesses named per query

A median of about six — and just 2–3 for local services. A shortlist, not ten links.

Reviews
decide local recommendations

Every local query recommended the businesses with the highest star ratings and most reviews — explicitly.

3rd-party
sources get you named

Picks were sourced to independent review sites, not the businesses' own websites.

0
named for a vague query

One ambiguous B2B query named zero businesses. Unclear category = AI can't recommend you.

What it means for business owners

To be the business AI recommends, you must be unambiguously categorized, well-reviewed, clearly located, and — above all — written about by independent sources the models trust. Ranking on Google was about being on the list. Winning in AI is about being the recommendation.
Method: 8 natural "recommend me…" prompts run through Perplexity in one U.S. session on July 30, 2026; each result logged verbatim (count of businesses named, citations, entities). This is a pilot — one engine, small sample, one location, one point in time — being scaled across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews and multiple markets.
