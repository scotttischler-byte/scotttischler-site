Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how scotttischler.com ("we," "us," or "the site"), operated by Scott Tischler, collects, uses, and protects information when you visit the site or contact us. We aim to be straightforward: we collect only what helps us run the site and respond to you, and we do not sell your personal information.

Information we collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Information you provide. When you submit the contact form, the franchise/partnership form, or the newsletter signup, we receive the information you enter — such as your name, email address, company, and message.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we and our analytics providers may automatically collect limited technical data such as pages viewed, referring website, approximate location (derived from IP address), device and browser type, and the date and time of your visit. This is collected through cookies and similar technologies, and only after you consent where required.

Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar tools to understand how visitors use the site and to improve it. Where required by law, these tools load only after you accept them via our cookie banner; if you decline, non-essential analytics and advertising tools are not loaded. We may use providers such as Google Analytics and advertising/retargeting pixels (for example, Meta or Google Ads) to measure traffic and, where enabled, to show relevant ads to people who have visited the site. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiries and follow up on franchise, partnership, speaking, or collaboration requests.
  • To send occasional updates if you subscribed to the newsletter (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To understand site usage and improve content and performance.
  • Where enabled and consented, to measure and improve advertising.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate the site (such as our hosting and form provider, analytics providers, and email tools), and only as needed for those services, or when required by law.

Your choices and rights

You can decline non-essential cookies through the banner, unsubscribe from emails at any time, and request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have provided. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK) or CCPA/CPRA (California), including the right to opt out of certain processing. To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below.

Data retention and security

We keep the information you provide only as long as needed to respond to you and for legitimate business or legal purposes, and we take reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email scott@airecommend.ai.

This policy is provided as a general template and describes the site's current practices. It is not legal advice. For a policy tailored to your specific obligations and the exact tools you enable, please have it reviewed by qualified legal counsel.
