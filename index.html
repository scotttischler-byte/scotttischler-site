About

Two decades turning attention into authority.

A marketing-technology operator now working on the frontier of how people — and AI — decide who to trust.

When someone asks an AI which business is best, my work makes sure the right answer is the one that gets said.

I'm Scott Tischler, Founder and Chairman of AIrecommend.ai. My focus is the intersection of generative AI and practical business growth — building the entity authority and structured knowledge that answer engines reward, so the businesses I work with become the recommendation, not just a result.

Across 20+ years I've worked in marketing technology, AI innovation, and scaling digital businesses, with earlier chapters at American Express, MetLife, and UBS. I keep sharpening the craft through professional certificate programs at Harvard, Wharton, Oxford, and Stanford (plus edX and Coursera), and I studied law at Concord Law School — and I've taught, too, as an adjunct professor at Virginia Tech.

I'm also a longtime real estate investor and creative-finance practitioner. I bought my first property in 1998, read the top of the market and sold my portfolio ahead of the 2008 crash, then got back in when the numbers made sense again — with deals ranging up to a 48,000-square-foot warehouse. I was renting by the room — the co-living model the market now calls "PadSplit" — years before it had a name.

Away from the desk, I've spent decades in the gym and at the jeweler's bench — fitness and jewelry design being lifelong pursuits in discipline and taste.
