The Maker

Thirty years, one idea

Founder & Master Jeweler

Designed and invented at the bench

Diamond Locs was founded by a jeweler with thirty years of practice. The Gem-Loc began as a simple observation: locs have been adorned for generations, yet the world of fine jewelry had never built a piece for them.

So it did. The Gem-Loc is the founder's own design and invention, made with the same care and standards as any piece of fine jewelry.
