A new category

For as long as locs have been worn, they have been adorned. Diamond Locs brings the standards of the jewelry bench to the loc: real stones, real settings, and a clasp engineered to be worn every day.

The Gem-Loc™

A clasp that is jewelry first.

At its center, a bezel-set stone. Around it, pavé. Along the edge, a fine hinge line, so the piece opens, closes around the loc, and reads as a single, finished object.

It can be made as a quiet band of white diamonds or as a full sculptural piece. A butterfly, a fairy, a companion. The same clasp carries them all.
