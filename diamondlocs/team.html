The Team

The people behind the Gem-Loc

A jeweler, an operator, counsel and a voice in the community.

Founder & Designer

Scott Tischler

Scott is a jeweler of thirty years who designed and invented the Gem-Loc. He designs fine jewelry, from custom commissions to an 18K gold line, and leads every design at Diamond Locs. Beyond the bench, he is Chairman and Chief AI Officer of AIrecommend.ai and has more than two decades of executive leadership across financial services, academia and technology.

CEO & Founder

Kathy P. Carr

Kathy is an entrepreneur, operator and community leader. She is Chief Executive Officer of Skyview Business Consulting, the parent company of AIrecommend.ai, AIFranchise.ai and Dorvo.ai, and she starts companies and scales them, with a focus on operations, franchising and partnerships.

Chief Legal Counsel

Roy Waddell

Roy is a former judge and attorney with experience in corporate, franchise and intellectual-property law. He guides Diamond Locs on legal matters, including the protection of the company and its designs.

Influencer Liaison

Limor

Limor builds Diamond Locs’ relationships with creators and influencers. Her strongest ground is beauty and aesthetics, and she is the first call for anyone who wants to wear the Gem-Loc and share it.
