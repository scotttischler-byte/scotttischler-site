The Craft

Made at the bench

Not assembled. Not cast and shipped. Designed, set and finished by hand.

Setting

Stones set to stay

A piece that lives in hair and moves with a life has to be built differently from a ring that sits on a finger. Every stone is set with that in mind: secure beads, even pavé, and a clean finish that will not catch.

The hinge line is part of the design, not an afterthought. It is drawn, cut and fitted so the clasp closes true.

The process

From sketch to wear

I

Design

Your concept is drawn and refined until the proportions, the stones and the scale are right for the loc it will wear.

II

Selection

Stones are chosen by hand for color, cut and fire, then matched across the piece so the pavé reads as one surface.

III

Fabrication

The clasp and its figure are built, fitted and set at the bench, with every moving part tested before a stone goes in.

IV

Finishing

Polished, inspected under magnification and fitted to you. Each piece leaves in a presentation case.

A promise

If a stone ever loosens, we reset it. A Gem-Loc is made to be worn, and to be kept.

Terms of care and repair to be confirmed at launch.
