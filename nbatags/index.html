Questions

Good to know

Is the wood really from an NBA court?

Yes. Our first run is cut from a panel of the 2011 NBA All-Star Game court, sourced through NBALAB. When the panel is gone, that run is over.

Do I pay when I reserve?

No. Reserving is free and does not charge a card. You will get an email when the Kickstarter opens.

Can I reserve more than one team?

Yes. Add as many team and finish combinations as you like.

What finishes are there?

Silver (polished stainless steel), Black (black-plated stainless steel) and 24K Gold. Every team comes in all three.

How much do they cost?

Final pricing is announced with the Kickstarter launch. People who reserve hear first.

When will they ship?

Shipping dates are set when the campaign launches and funds. Production starts once the campaign funds.
