The wood

A court, reclaimed

What the tags are made of, and why there is only so much of it.

Real hardwood

Maple that was played on

NBA courts are laid in maple and retired after years of play. We reclaim that wood and cut it to fit the back of every tag.

Our first run comes from a panel of the 2011 NBA All-Star Game court, sourced through NBALAB. When the panel is gone, that run is over.

Built to wear

Steel, set by jewelers

The wood sits in a stainless steel frame. The team mark is printed on the front. The tag is designed by working jewelers, and it is built for daily wear.

Silver, black-plated or 24K gold finish. A gift box with a team sticker on the lid.

Upcycled

Reclaimed wood that would otherwise be scrapped, turned into something a fan can wear.
